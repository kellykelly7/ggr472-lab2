Typical Wednesdays featuring Kelly, Human Geography and French Linguistics major with a GIS minor

Usually, I take the typical amount of courses during the school year to avoid burnout and fatigue, but surprise surprise, this semester, Winter 2025 here at the University of Toronto I am currently taking 6 courses purely out of interest, and this is where I should express that curiosity really does kill the cat, or in this case, kills the amount of the time I have left for sleeping. In the map to the left, we can see exactly where I need to go in order to make it around campus to all of the courses, featuring my busiest day of the week: Wednesdays. For starters, I get off the subway at Museum Station (small box in Queen's Park, also an endpoint in the path) and make my way to J. M. Kelly Library (big square, south east) for a morning study session, given that I have class at 10, and typically make it downtown by 9:15. After going to class at Carr hall (skinny rectangle), I sometimes have class over at Victoria College (funky pointy block, north east), before which I would usually go to E. J. Pratt Library (square, south of Victoria College) to work on pending assignments in the meantime. Afterwards, I would head on over to Robarts Library (triangular large building, west) to continue to work on my assignments until 3 o'clock, which is the lecture time for GGR472: Developing Web Maps down at Sidney Smith Hall (sort of T-shaped building). To end the day off, I would then rush home via St. George Station (northernmost polygon on the little map) in order to catch my 6-8 online lecture for GGR372: GIS for Public Health. The path (as I have yet to figure out how to add arrows), is a visual representation of a busy Wednesdays but excludes the optional side-quest over to E.J Pratt Library and Victoria College.
